FAQs

Questions we hear most

Can't find your answer? Call the lab and we will help.

Do I need to fast before my test?

Only for some tests. Fasting blood sugar needs 8 to 10 hours and a lipid profile needs 9 to 12 hours. Water is fine. CBC, thyroid, HbA1c and most other blood tests need no fasting.

How will I receive my report?

Reports are sent to your phone on WhatsApp and to your email, and you can also collect a printed copy from the lab counter. Each report shows every value against its reference range.

How long do reports take?

Most routine blood and urine tests are reported the same day. Cultures take 2 to 3 days and biopsies take 3 to 7 days.

Can I take my regular medicines before the test?

In most cases yes. Tell the phlebotomist what you take, and follow your doctor's advice if you were asked to hold any medicine.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Many tests can be booked directly. A prescription helps us pick the right tests, and your doctor is the best person to interpret the results.

How does home collection work?

Choose a slot in the booking form and send us the message. We call to confirm the time, and the phlebotomist arrives with sterile single-use kits and labels the tubes in front of you.

Still have a question?

Call or message the lab and we will help.
