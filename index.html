Why "Trinetri"

Three eyes on every sample

Trinetri means three-eyed. The lab's workflow has three separate checks, each done by a different person.

About the lab
CHECK 1 OF 3

Pre-analytical

Everything before testing. The sample is labelled in front of you, drawn with sterile single-use kits and kept at the right temperature on the way to the lab.

CHECK 2 OF 3

Analytical

The testing itself. Machines are calibrated and run daily controls, and a technician reviews any result outside the expected pattern and repeats it if needed.

CHECK 3 OF 3

Post-analytical

Everything after testing. A pathologist reads and signs the report before release, adding comments where a value needs attention.
