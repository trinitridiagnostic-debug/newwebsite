2. What we provide

We provide laboratory testing and a home sample collection service. Our website lets you search tests, see prices and send a booking request. Test reports support a doctor's assessment and are not a diagnosis on their own. Please discuss your results with your doctor.

3. Booking and confirmation

  • You can send a booking request through the website, which opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, or by calling us.
  • A booking is confirmed only after we contact you to agree the date, time slot and address.
  • Slots depend on availability. We may ask you to choose another slot, and we will tell you if we cannot serve your address.

4. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Indian rupees (INR). Test prices on the website are the maximum retail price (MRP) from our price list. The final amount is confirmed when you book.
  • Offer prices on packages apply as shown on the offer and may be withdrawn or changed. An offer price already paid for will be honoured.
  • Home sample collection is free where the website says so.
  • You can pay online by UPI, card or net banking through our payment partner Razorpay, or pay at collection when that option is offered. We do not see or store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details. They are handled by Razorpay and your bank.
  • A payment is complete only when you see the confirmation and a payment ID. Keep that ID for any query.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Give correct name, mobile number, address and test details.
  • Follow the preparation instructions for your tests, such as fasting. Wrong preparation can change results.
  • Be available at the address during the booked slot. If you are not, we may need to rebook.

6. Samples and reports

  • Reports are usually ready on the same day for routine tests. This is an estimate, not a guarantee. Some tests are sent to partner laboratories (shown as out-lab tests) and take longer.
  • We may ask for a fresh sample if the first one is unsuitable, for example if it is clotted, too little or wrongly stored.
  • Reports are shared on WhatsApp or email, or can be collected at the lab counter. We treat reports as confidential, as set out in our Privacy Policy.
