Cancellation and Refunds

When you can cancel a booking and how refunds are paid.

Terms and ConditionsPrivacy PolicyShipping PolicyCancellation and RefundsContact Us

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Summary

You can cancel a booking before your sample is collected. If you paid online and we cannot provide the service, you get a refund to the same payment method. Please read the details below.

1. Cancelling a booking

  • You can cancel or reschedule free of charge until 2 hours before your slot. Call or WhatsApp us on +91 91873 15972.
  • Once the phlebotomist has set out for your address, or after your sample is collected, the booking cannot be cancelled because the sample and kits are already in use.
  • If you cancel in time and had paid online, you get a full refund.

2. When you get a refund

  • You cancelled in time, as described above.
  • We cannot collect your sample or provide the test, for example no slot is available or your address is outside our service area.
  • You were charged twice for the same booking.
  • Your sample was unsuitable because of our error and you do not want a recollection. A free recollection is offered first.
  • Your payment was deducted but the booking was not completed.

3. When a refund is not available

  • The sample has been collected and testing has begun, except in the cases listed above.
  • The report has been issued.
  • The sample was unsuitable because of something outside our control, such as not following fasting instructions. We will offer to recollect, and a new collection may be charged.
  • Different results than you expected. Results reflect your sample. Please talk to your doctor about unexpected results.

4. How refunds are paid

  • Approved refunds go back to the same payment method used for the payment, such as the same UPI ID, card or bank account.
  • We start the refund within 2 working days of approval. Your bank or card issuer then usually takes 5 to 7 working days to show it.
  • If you paid at collection, any refund due is returned in the same way you paid.

5. If your money was debited but the booking failed

Failed online payments are usually returned automatically to your account within 5 to 7 working days. If you do not receive it, contact us with your payment ID, name and mobile number and we will follow it up with Razorpay.

6. How to ask for a cancellation or refund

Call or WhatsApp us on +91 91873 15972 and give your name, mobile number, booking date and payment ID. We will reply with the outcome. Opening hours: Monday to Saturday 7:00 am to 10:00 pm, Sunday 7:00 am to 2:00 pm.
