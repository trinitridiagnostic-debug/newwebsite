Shipping Policy

How home sample collection and report delivery work.

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Our service is not a shipped product

Trinetri Diagnostics provides laboratory testing. We do not sell or ship physical goods. This page explains how our home sample collection and report delivery work, which is what "shipping" means for a lab.

1. Home sample collection

  • Book on this website or call us. We call back to confirm your date, time slot and address.
  • Home sample collection is free where the website says so.
  • Time slots we offer: 7:00 to 9:00 am, 9:00 to 11:00 am, 11:00 am to 1:00 pm and 4:00 to 6:00 pm. Our phlebotomist arrives with sterile single-use kits and labels each tube in front of you.
  • We serve Betageri and nearby areas. Tell us your address when you book and we will confirm whether we can reach you. If we cannot, you are welcome to visit the lab.
  • If you are not available during the slot, call us and we will rebook a new time at no extra charge.

2. Delivery of reports

  • Reports are delivered digitally on WhatsApp or email, or you can collect a copy at the lab counter during opening hours.
  • Most routine tests are ready on the same day. Tests sent to partner laboratories (out-lab tests) take longer, and we will tell you the expected time when you book.
  • Reports are shared only with the number or email you give us.

3. No physical shipping charges

Because nothing is shipped, there are no shipping or delivery charges. The price you pay is the price of the test or package.

4. If something goes wrong

  • If a sample cannot be collected or is unsuitable, we will arrange a recollection. See our Cancellation and Refunds policy for refunds.
  • If your report is late, call or WhatsApp us on +91 91873 15972 and we will give you an update.
