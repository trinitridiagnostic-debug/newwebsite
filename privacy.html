Privacy Policy

How Trinetri Diagnostics collects, uses and protects your information.

Our promise

Your health information is private. This policy explains what Trinetri Diagnostics collects when you use this website or book with us, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, mobile number, address, preferred date and time, and the tests or package you choose.
  • Health information: your samples, test results and any notes you give us, when you use our lab services.
  • Payment details: if you pay online, Razorpay collects your payment details. We receive only the payment status, amount and payment ID. We never see or store your card number, UPI PIN or net banking password.
  • On your device: this website saves your selected tests and a few display choices in your browser's local storage so your selection is remembered between pages. It stays on your device. We do not use advertising cookies or sell your data.

2. How we use it

  • To confirm and carry out your booking, collect samples, run tests and deliver reports.
  • To contact you by call or WhatsApp about your booking or report.
  • To process payments and refunds.
  • To meet legal and medical record-keeping duties.
  • To improve our service. We do not use your health information for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

  • Our phlebotomists and lab staff, only as needed to serve you.
  • Partner laboratories, when a test you ordered is processed outside our lab.
  • Razorpay and your bank, to process online payments.
  • WhatsApp, when you choose to send your booking request there. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

4. How long we keep it

We keep reports and booking records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet medical record and legal requirements. After that we delete or anonymise them.

5. How we protect it

Only authorised staff can see your reports. Payments run through Razorpay's secure systems. No method of sending data over the internet is perfectly secure, so please share your reports only with people you trust.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. Some records must be kept by law, and we will tell you if that applies. You can clear the data this website stores on your device at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Contact us using the details below to make a request.

7. Children

If a patient is under 18, please book and share details through a parent or guardian.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows the latest version.
